Privacy Notice

Last updated: 19 November 2026

How This Portfolio Handles Contact Information

This plain-language notice describes information handled by the contact form on Leonard Juma’s personal portfolio. It is general information, not legal advice; the final public policy should be checked against the laws and hosting setup that apply to the deployed website.

Information You Submit

If you use the contact form, it asks for your name, email address, subject, and message. You choose whether to submit that information. Please do not include passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information in a general enquiry.

Why It Is Used

The information is used to read and respond to your enquiry, follow up on a project or opportunity you ask about, and keep the correspondence needed to manage that conversation. This portfolio does not request contact details for an unrelated account or newsletter feature.

Storage and Access

The contact form sends its fields to a Django backend, which stores submitted messages in the application database. The local development configuration uses SQLite. A production deployment may use a different hosting or database configuration; the actual deployment environment determines where its data is stored. The site owner may access messages to respond to enquiries and administer the site.

No fixed message-retention period has been specified. Messages should be kept only as long as needed for the enquiry and any applicable record-keeping requirement, and removed when no longer needed.

Third-Party Services and Technical Requests

The pages may request web fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser loads an external resource, that provider may receive ordinary technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and the WhatsApp channel take you to those providers and their own privacy practices. This portfolio does not control those external services.

Sharing and Security

Contact information is not published as part of the site content. It may be disclosed if required by law or needed to address a valid legal request. Reasonable care should be taken to protect stored messages, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Use the form only for information you are comfortable sending.

Your Requests

You may ask what contact information you submitted, request a correction, or ask for a message to be deleted by emailing leon.jumao@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify the relevant enquiry, but do not send additional sensitive information. Applicable privacy rights and response requirements depend on the law that applies to the site and requester.

Changes and Contact

This notice may be revised when the contact form, data storage, hosting, or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies its latest revision. Questions about this notice or a contact-form submission can be sent to leon.jumao@gmail.com. Leonard Juma is based in Kisii, Kenya.
